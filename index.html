Screen C · In your own words

Tell your twin what matters.

There aren't right answers here. Say as much or as little as you want.

C1
Describe your life right now, and what's on your mind most these days.

Your days, where you grew up, what's shaped how you see the world, and your biggest concerns right now.

C2
Of the things in your life, what could the government do that would help you most?

Think about: money, home, work, family, health, safety, your community

C3
Looking beyond your own life, what bothers you most about how the country is run?

Think about: the economy, taxes and who pays them, how government is run and who it listens to, rights and freedoms, the country's direction
